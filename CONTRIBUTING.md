# Contributing

The most useful help is knowledge from your own job search.

- **Add job boards for your country or profession** in `blank/config/sources.md`, including whether they need an account.
- **Add search phrases** in `blank/config/queries.md`, especially in languages other than English.
- **Add a form trick** to `AGENT_BRIEF.md` if you found a site where forms behave strangely.
- **Translate** the README or suggest better wording for `SETUP.md`.
- **Report where setup got stuck.** Say which part, what you saw, and which operating system you use.

Before you send anything, check it holds no personal data: no real names, phone numbers, emails, CVs or application records.

Please keep the rules in `CLAUDE.md` strict. Changes that remove a safety rule, such as getting past CAPTCHAs, creating accounts or submitting forms that require a personal certification, won't be accepted.
