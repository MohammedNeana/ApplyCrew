DO
- Short sentences. Contractions (I'm, I've, didn't). First person.
- Concrete specifics from the CV: a real product, a real number from the CV, a real tool or client. One small honest detail beats three adjectives.
- Mention the company's actual product, service or mission once.
- Vary openings. Not every answer starts with "I".
- 2-4 sentences for short-answer boxes. Cover letters max ~150 words, 3 short paragraphs.
- Slight informality is fine: "Happy to jump on a call whenever suits you."

NEVER use (instant AI tells)
- em-dashes (—), "delve", "leverage", "passionate", "thrilled", "excited to", "I am confident that", "furthermore",
  "moreover", "in today's fast-paced", "seamless", "robust", "cutting-edge", "spearheaded", "synergy", "tapestry",
  "testament", "align with your mission", "I hope this finds you well", "I would be a great fit", "dynamic",
  "innovative solutions", "proven track record", bullet lists inside a free-text answer, "Not only... but also".
- Restating the question. Summing up at the end. Exclamation marks.

FACTS (never invent anything beyond these)
- Use only what's in profile/candidate_profile.md / the CV. Years of experience: the number in the profile. No made-up metrics, employers, titles or tools.
- Availability: use the notice period / start date in profile/qa_bank.md. Do NOT state or imply the owner is currently employed or unemployed unless qa_bank.md says what
  to write. If a REQUIRED question asks "Are you currently employed?" or "current salary" and qa_bank.md has no answer, stop that application and log it in tracker/needs_me.md.
- Salary if asked: "Negotiable" when text is allowed; if a number is required use salary_defaults below.
- Work permission, nationality, languages, education: exactly as in profile/qa_bank.md and candidate_profile.md.
